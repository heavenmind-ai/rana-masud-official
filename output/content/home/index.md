# Rana Masud

[Rana Masud](https://ranamasudbd.com/biography/)

(Bengali: রানা মাসুদ) (born 21 September 1979) is a Bangladeshi Film Director and producer. In his career, he has directed nearly three hundred (300) AD Films. He has established a production house named Ferywala Communications and concentrated on developing audiovisual promotional material for both brand and social communication. Working as a teacher at the Bangladesh Film Institute (BFI) for the last 08 years. Rana Masud Film Director, Producer, Theatre Activist, and Teacher in Bangladesh.
